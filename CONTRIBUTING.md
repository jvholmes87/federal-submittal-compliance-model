# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve the public research artifact without introducing operational or identifying information.

## Appropriate contributions

- Corrections to terminology, equations, diagrams, or methodological statements
- References to relevant peer-reviewed literature
- Suggestions for measurement validity, research design, or generalization
- Improvements to documentation structure and reproducibility
- Public examples that are independently sourced and do not expose restricted operational data

## Do not submit

- Project, task-order, contract, building, installation, unit, or contractor identifiers
- Government or contractor personnel names, contact information, correspondence, or screenshots
- Raw operational data, reviewer comments, government forms, internal links, or payment information
- Controlled unclassified information, export-controlled material, credentials, tokens, or secrets
- Claims that the current evidence establishes causality, general prevalence, or doctoral novelty

## Research claims

Every quantitative claim should retain its numerator, denominator, unit of analysis, population, date, and source. Historical metrics with different definitions must not be combined into a single trend without a defensible common measurement rule.

Literature suggestions should include a stable citation or DOI when available. Established field terminology should be preferred over new internal labels unless the contribution explains why existing terminology is insufficient.

## Pull requests

Keep pull requests focused and explain:

1. What changed.
2. Why it changed.
3. What source or reasoning supports it.
4. Whether the change affects a metric definition, research limitation, or sanitization boundary.

By contributing, you agree that accepted documentation contributions may be distributed under the repository's CC BY 4.0 license.
